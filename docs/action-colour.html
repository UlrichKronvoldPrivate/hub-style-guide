What the colour has to survive

Five skies in two themes, and four neighbours it may not be mistaken for: flare (magenta), moss (success), amber (warning), rust (danger). That rules out every orange, red, pink and green. What is left is blue, violet, and no colour at all.

Contrast, day on glass

Spruce 14:1. Panton 8.3:1. Skagen 5.3:1. Cobalt 7.9:1. All pass AA for text; Skagen is the only one that would fail AAA for small text, which matters for quiet buttons and links.

What changes if you pick Spruce

Links get an underline, because ink alone is not an affordance. Focus rings stay ink by day and frost by night, offset 3px so they sit on the glass, not on the button. Everything else is a one-token change.
