Outlines, not lit edges

Lucent tiles are frosted and drawn with a 1px hairline in ink. The inner highlight is gone. Separation comes from the outline and from tone, the way Nothing's widgets sit on a wallpaper.

A widget module

Four columns, 132px rows, 12px gutters. Tiles are 1×1, 2×1, 4×2 — never freeform. Hero figure spans two; the chart spans four. Tiles differ by size and role, not by decoration.

Dot matrix is the accent

Doto appears only as readouts: the hero figure, tile numerals, timestamps, the chart's axis. Geist carries everything else. Body text never goes dotted — that is what 5.0 fixed about 4.0.

The chart is drawn in the same matrix

Bars are columns of dots on the 8px module, idle ones in outline grey, today in Skagen. The chart and the readouts share one material instead of a chart looking like a chart.

Control grammar

Text actions are pills. Icon actions are circles. Selection is a filled ink pill. Choices are ring-then-dot. One shape per job, which is why it reads as a system.

What it is not

Not black-and-white: the sky and seeds stay, tonal tints stay. Not tracked-out capitals as eyebrows — readouts do that job. Not Nothing's typefaces: Geist is theirs, the dot face is ours, the content is Danish statistics.
