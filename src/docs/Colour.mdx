# Colour

Every value below is read off the live document, so this page shows the true
token value in whichever theme and seed the toolbar is set to. Switch **Theme**
to Winter night and **Seed** between glazes, and the swatches follow.

## The sky

The page ground is five radial stops of glaze over porcelain by day and over
spruce-black by night. `--hub-sky-1` leads at top-left, `-2` right, `-3`
low-right, `-4` is the horizon, `-5` a centre haze. A seed reorders them and
touches nothing else.

## Tonal glass

Surfaces are white at five opacities by day and frost at five by night. Each

## Glazes

The five sky stops and clay, as flat values. Use them as a **tonal cell** —
a stop of the sky at `--hub-tint-alpha` over the glass — for stat rows, empty
states and node categories. Never as a solid field on a component.

## Voltage

Cobalt is the current on surfaces: interaction, focus, edges, selection. Flare
is the accent that appears once on a screen, or not at all. Together they stay
under a twentieth of the surfaces; the sky does not count.

## Status

Separate from voltage, always. The accent never stands in for state, and state
never borrows the accent — which is why danger is a rust red rather than a
brighter version of flare.

## Three theme states and five seeds

Only semantic tokens change between themes; primitives never do, and components
never restyle inside a theme block. There are three states to design for: an
explicit `data-theme="light"`, an explicit `data-theme="dark"`, and the
un-stamped default where only `prefers-color-scheme` separates them. Seeds sit
on top of all three: `data-seed` reorders the five sky stops and nothing else,
so a seed is safe to set once per app and forget.
